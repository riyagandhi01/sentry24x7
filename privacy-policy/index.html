1. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the user's role and the Sentry24x7 features being used.

Account and Profile Information

When a user registers for or uses Sentry24x7, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • User role
  • Society or organization information
  • Account verification and approval information

Users may be required to verify their email address. Account or society registration may also require approval by an authorized society administrator or Sentry24x7 administrator.

Society and Security Management Information

Depending on the features used, Sentry24x7 may process information related to:

  • Societies
  • Residents
  • Secretaries and administrators
  • Supervisors
  • Security guards
  • Visitors
  • Vehicles
  • Gates
  • Patrol routes and checkpoints
  • Security incidents and reports
  • Access and movement records
  • Patrol records

2. Location Information

Sentry24x7 may collect precise location information when location-based functionality is used.

For Guard Patrol Tracking, location information is used to:

  • Track an active security patrol
  • Monitor patrol progress
  • Record the guard's position during an active patrol
  • Verify movement relative to assigned patrol routes and checkpoints
  • Detect when a guard moves outside the assigned patrol route
  • Generate patrol-related alerts and operational records

Background Location

During an active Guard Patrol, Sentry24x7 may collect precise location information even when the app is closed, running in the background, or not actively in use.

Background location is used to maintain continuity of the active patrol, monitor patrol progress, record the guard's position and detect deviations from the assigned patrol route.

Important: Background location is used for Guard Patrol Tracking and is not used for advertising purposes.

Users can manage location permissions through their Android device settings. Restricting location permissions may prevent Guard Patrol Tracking or related features from operating correctly.

3. Camera, Microphone and Media

Sentry24x7 may request access to the device's camera and microphone when users use features requiring:

  • Photos
  • Video
  • Audio
  • QR-code scanning
  • Incident reporting
  • Other supported media functionality

Camera and microphone access is used only for application functionality requiring those capabilities.

4. Bluetooth and USB

Sentry24x7 may use Bluetooth and supported USB functionality to communicate with compatible security, gate-control, FASTag or related hardware.

These capabilities are used to provide supported Sentry24x7 hardware functionality.

5. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through Sentry24x7 to:

  • Register, authenticate and verify users
  • Manage user accounts
  • Register and manage societies
  • Provide role-based access
  • Manage residents, visitors and vehicles
  • Provide security and gate-management functionality
  • Operate Guard Patrol Tracking
  • Monitor patrol routes and checkpoints
  • Generate security and patrol alerts
  • Generate reports and operational records
  • Deliver application notifications
  • Provide application functionality
  • Maintain application and account security
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Prevent misuse

We do not use Guard Patrol background location for advertising purposes.

Firebase services used by the application may include functionality related to:

  • User authentication
  • Database storage
  • File or media storage
  • Cloud functionality
  • Push notifications

Information processed through Firebase may be processed by Google according to the applicable Firebase and Google privacy and security practices.

7. Sharing and Sale of Personal Information

Sentry24x7 does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared or made accessible where reasonably necessary:

  • To provide Sentry24x7 functionality
  • To authorized users or society administrators according to their roles and permissions

  • To maintain security and prevent misuse

Access to information within Sentry24x7 is controlled according to applicable user roles and society permissions.

8. Notifications

Sentry24x7 may send notifications related to application functionality, including security events, patrol activities, alerts, account activity and other relevant service information.

Users can manage notification permissions through their Android device settings.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through Sentry24x7 against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss or misuse.

However, no application, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials.

10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Sentry24x7 services and for legitimate operational, security, contractual, fraud-prevention, legal or regulatory purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Certain security, access, patrol, transaction or operational records may need to be retained after an account is deleted where retention is reasonably necessary for security, legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention or legitimate record-keeping purposes.

11. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Sentry24x7 account and associated personal information.

Account deletion can be initiated from the Sentry24x7 application or through: https://sentry24x7.com/delete-account

Users may be required to verify their identity and account ownership before a deletion request is processed.

After successful verification and confirmation, the user's account and associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized as appropriate, except for information that must or may legitimately be retained for security, legal, regulatory, fraud-prevention, contractual or record-keeping purposes.

Any information retained after account deletion will be retained only for the applicable purpose and for as long as reasonably necessary.

For assistance with account deletion, users may contact: contact@sentry24x7.com
